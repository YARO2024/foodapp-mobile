Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-19

FoodApp is a mobile app for food logging and nutrient intake tracking. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

1. Who we are

The data controller for this policy is the developer of FoodApp (“we”).

FoodApp is not a medical service and does not replace professional healthcare advice.

2. Summary

  • Most data stays on your device only (local storage).
  • We do not sell personal data and do not operate a cloud user account.
  • Network access is used only when you use search, barcode, photo recognition, or voice input features.

3. Data we process

Profile (optional): display name, age, sex, height, weight, nutrition goal, activity level, focus path.

Nutrition data: meal entries, portions, nutrients, water, supplements, daily history.

App settings: language, theme, reminders, pinned nutrients, custom macro targets.

Profile photo (optional): avatar image stored locally on device.

Technical data: app version, local calendar day marker — on device only.

4. Device permissions

Camera — barcode scanning and food photos when you use those features. No background camera use.

Photo library — choosing a profile avatar.

Microphone & speech recognition — voice meal input when enabled. We do not store separate audio recordings on our servers.

Notifications — local meal/water reminders if you enable them.

5. Third-party services

Data is sent to external services only when you initiate the feature:

  • Open Food Facts — barcode sent to fetch product nutrition (world.openfoodfacts.org).
  • USDA FoodData Central — search query text when you use online food search (fdc.nal.usda.gov).
  • OpenAI (optional) — if an API key is configured, food photos may be sent for dish recognition. Without a key, photos stay on device.

We do not use ad SDKs, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

6. Legal bases (GDPR / EEA)

  • Performance of the app / your request — logging and displaying nutrition data.
  • Consent — camera, photos, microphone, notifications.
  • Legitimate interest — keeping the app secure and functional without profiling.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is stored in local device storage (AsyncStorage) until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling the app removes local data unless your OS backup restores it separately.

You can reset the current day in the app; full profile/history reset — Settings → “Delete all data”, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing the contact below.

EEA/UK residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA without parental consent). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

10. International transfers

When using Open Food Facts, USDA, or OpenAI, requests may be processed on servers outside your country. Scope is limited as described in section 5.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when app features change. The current version is available in-app and at this URL. The effective date is at the top.
